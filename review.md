This section is reproduced from the initial research audit. It records searches for the original asymptotic construction; it is not a completed independent priority audit of the later finite counterexample. Historical priority remains unresolved.

## Originality investigation repeated after discovery

The repeated searches covered the published number and title, both authors, slope-minus-one segments, x+y=2, quasiconvexity, negative curvature, Tutte edge replacements, high-girth graph limits, and Japanese as well as English search terms for convexity and counterexamples. Resources queried include arXiv, publisher and institutional repositories, Zenodo, Preprints.org, MathDB, Hexagon, Erdős Problems, ProbXiv, and GitHub. Several direct repository pages were cached snapshots or unavailable. A site-restricted search giving no relevant record carries little evidentiary weight.

**The 2019 lead.** On 30 May 2019 Hidefumi Hiraishi presented *Smallest Counterexamples for Convexity and Log-concavity of the Tutte Polynomial* at the Hungarian–Japanese Symposium in Tokyo, with Hiroshi Imai, Sonoko Moriyama, Shuma Okamura and Shinya Shiroshita as coauthors of the paper. The project's report, section 4(3), says the authors found a counterexample to convexity under a linear relation between the variables in a regime some distance from the conventional one. It gives neither the line equation nor the graph hypotheses. The next paragraph discusses simplicity for the separate coefficient log-concavity problem. Transferring that sentence to the convexity result would misread the report. The public program lists a title and time. The conference site and author records located here do not provide the paper's statement. Japan's National Diet Library lists the volume under call number M18-B1685. Whether their counterexample lies on x+y=2 in its nonnegative segment for a simple 2-connected graph is unresolved. This blocks a claim of first resolution under the requested standard.
